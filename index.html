The Evangelion Wiki is an editable encyclopedia about the Evangelion franchise . Our wiki aims to bring together all information about the Evangelion anime series, films, and related media. You can help us by editing or adding interesting facts to the articles, of which we currently have 790.

Evangelion Pilots (Children)

  • Shinji Ikari is the protagonist and the Third Children, piloting Unit-01. He is an insecure teenager who seeks his father's approval.

  • Rei Ayanami is the First Child and pilot of Unit-00. A silent, mysterious girl of artificial origin, she has close ties to the head of NERV.

  • Asuka Langley Soryu (Shikinami in rebuilds) is the Second Children and pilot of Unit-02. A proud and hot-tempered girl from Germany with a difficult childhood.

  • Kaworu Nagisa is the Fifth Children (original) and the seventeenth Angel, possessing a deep philosophical understanding of human nature.

  • is the Fourth Children, Shinji's classmate who pilots Unit-03 at a critical moment.Mari Illustrious Makinami is a cheerful female pilot who appears in the Evangelion Rebuild series.

  • Kensuke Aida is a fictional character from the Neon Genesis Evangelion franchise. He is an military otaku, Toji's best friend, and one of the "Other Children," Eva pilot candidates in the same class as Shinji Ikari, Toji Suzuhara, Rei Ayanami, Asuka Langley Sohryu, and Hikari Horaki.

NERV staff and other heroes

  1. Gendo Ikari is the commander of NERV and Shinji's cold father, who pursues his own hidden agenda.

  2. Misato Katsuragi is NERV's Director of Operations and has become Shinji and Asuka's caretaker in everyday life.

  3. Ritsuko Akagi is the chief researcher and creator of the robot's technological base.

  4. Ryoji Kaji is a double agent and security specialist who unravels the organization's conspiracies.
